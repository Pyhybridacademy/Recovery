What is KYC verification?

Learn about our verification process

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is a regulatory requirement that helps us:

  • Verify we're working with legitimate victims
  • Comply with anti-fraud and anti-money laundering laws
  • Protect both parties from fraudulent activities

We verify your identity with government-issued ID, address proof, and a selfie. The process is quick, secure, and conducted through our encrypted platform.
